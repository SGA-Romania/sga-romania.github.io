Welcome to the SGA Romania Student Chapter

We're a student chapter built around curiosity, geoscience, and the simple idea that rocks, minerals, and the Earth beneath our feet are far more interesting than they might seem at first glance.

We bring together students who want to learn, explore, and share what they find interesting - from minerals and crystallography to geology, geophysics, and the processes that shape our planet.

Through talks, workshops, field trips, research activities, and plenty of opportunities to get our hands dirty, we want to make geoscience something you can experience, not just study from a textbook.

Come explore with us.
